### Coursicle

**Overview:**
Coursicle is the largest course notification service with the widest university coverage. They focus on schedule planning features alongside notifications.

**Pricing Tiers:**

1. **Free Tier - $0**
   - Track 1-2 sections (varies by school)
   - Basic email notifications
   - Standard check intervals (~30 min)
   - Schedule builder included
   - Ad-supported

2. **Coursicle Pro - $4.99/month or $14.99/semester**
   - Unlimited section tracking
   - Faster notifications (~5 min checks)
   - SMS notifications
   - Ad-free experience
   - Extended schedule planning features
   - Grade distribution data (where available)

**Strengths:**
- Largest university coverage (1,000+ schools)
- Schedule builder integration
- Well-established brand recognition
- Mobile apps (iOS/Android)

**Weaknesses:**
- Higher price point ($4.99/month vs SeatSteal's $2/month)
- Check frequency slower than SeatSteal Pro
- Limited analytics features
- Ads in free tier

---

### ClassRabbit

**Overview:**
ClassRabbit is a smaller player in the course notification market, focusing primarily on mid-size universities.

**Pricing Tiers:**

1. **Free Tier - $0**
   - Track 1-2 sections
   - Email notifications only
   - Standard check intervals (~30 min)
   - Limited university support

2. **Premium Tier - ~$3-5/month (varies)**
   - Track 5-10 sections
   - SMS notifications
   - Faster check intervals (~10 min)
   - Priority support

**Strengths:**
- Simple, focused product
- Competitive pricing in premium tier
- Good for specific universities they support

**Weaknesses:**
- Limited university coverage (~50 schools)
- Slower check frequency than competitors
- No enrollment analytics
- Less polished user experience
